# 3D icons

Source: **3dicons** — https://3dicons.co — by Vijay Verma (realvjy).
License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication). Free for personal and commercial use,
copying, editing and redistribution; attribution not required.

Original renders are 1000×1000 PNG on the author's CDN. The files here were downloaded in the
"color" style, cropped to their content and converted to transparent WebP at ≤220 px so the app
stays light. No other modification.

Icons used: headphone, mic, notebook, pencil, chat, target, medal, trophy, calendar, clock, star,
fire, tick, gift-box, rocket, bulb, music, video-cam, flash, puzzle, crown, chart, shield, folder,
lock.
